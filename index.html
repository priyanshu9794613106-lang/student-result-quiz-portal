ABOUT THE PORTAL

Your Academic Journey, Our Priority

Student Result & Quiz Portal is a web-based academic platform designed to provide students with a simple place to access results, attempt quizzes and manage their basic student information.

The project combines HTML, CSS and JavaScript concepts with responsive web design to create an interactive student portal.
